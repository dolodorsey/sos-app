# S.O.S. Atlanta Rollout — 90 Day Operating Plan

Status: EXECUTION
Start: 2026-08-25
Launch model: supply-first, zone-concentrated marketplace rollout
Primary market: Atlanta, Georgia

## Objective

Make S.O.S. reliably fulfill non-emergency roadside/mobile-vehicle requests in a deliberately constrained Atlanta launch area before expanding coverage.

## Production baseline — 2026-08-25

- Real S.O.S. users: 6
- Real Heroes: 0
- Verified real Heroes: 0
- Real Hero applications: 0
- Real recruiting candidates: 0
- Real missions: 0
- Waitlist: 0
- Atlanta service zones configured: 10
- Priority launch zones: Midtown, Buckhead, Hartsfield-Jackson Airport
- Production Vercel deployment: READY

Demo fixtures are not counted as supply or launch traction.

## Rollout doctrine

1. Recruit supply before buying meaningful customer demand.
2. Concentrate service into the first three priority zones.
3. Do not unlock a zone because the map exists; unlock it only when supply and quality gates are met.
4. Reward completed work and verified readiness, not registrations.
5. Track launch performance daily.
6. Expand only after reliable fulfillment is demonstrated.

## Phase 0 — Market ignition (Days 1-7)

### Supply
- Build a prospect list of at least 150 Atlanta roadside/mobile-auto operators.
- Target 50 completed Hero applications.
- Target 25 approved Heroes entering verification.
- Target 15 fully verified Heroes by end of the first activation sprint.
- Prioritize jump start, tire change, lockout, fuel delivery, battery service, towing and mobile mechanic capability.

### Priority zones
1. Midtown
2. Buckhead
3. Hartsfield-Jackson Airport

### Provider offer
- No bonus for account creation alone.
- Activation bonus requires verification + completed missions.
- Proposed launch quest: 5 completed missions = $50; 15 = additional $100; 30 = additional $200; quality bonus available at 4.8+ rating and target acceptance rate.
- Incentives remain draft until unit economics and payout operations are approved.

### Demand
- Build waitlist while provider supply is being activated.
- Do not run a broad paid campaign until minimum live-Hero coverage is reached.
- Start with owned audience, referrals, partner audiences, local communities and anchor B2B partners.

### B2B targets
- Apartment communities
- Parking facilities
- Hotels
- Colleges/universities
- Dealerships and used-car lots
- Fleet operators
- Delivery fleets
- Event/nightlife parking operations
- Property managers

## Phase 1 — Controlled beta (Days 8-21)

Launch only in GREEN zones.

### Initial zone gate
A zone is eligible for controlled demand when all are true:
- minimum verified Heroes met
- minimum live Heroes met
- payout/payment system healthy
- operator escalation path staffed
- target services have coverage

### Beta goals
- First 100 real customer requests
- >= 85% fulfillment
- >= 80% provider acceptance
- median ETA <= 25 minutes initially, moving toward <= 20
- cancellation <= 10%, moving toward <= 8%
- average customer rating >= 4.7
- zero unresolved critical safety events

### Customer launch hook
Working campaign concept: ATLANTA, YOU'RE NEVER STRANDED AGAIN.

Use a controlled first-service credit or category-specific launch offer rather than an uncapped free-service promise. Final value must be modeled against CAC, payout and service gross margin.

## Phase 2 — Core Atlanta density (Days 22-45)

When the initial zones remain GREEN:
- increase live-Hero targets during peak windows
- expand to Downtown/Centennial, West Midtown and East Atlanta/Kirkwood
- open provider referrals
- activate B2B pilot accounts
- begin membership conversion after successful missions
- create geo-specific acquisition campaigns by zone

## Phase 3 — Metro expansion (Days 46-90)

Unlock remaining Atlanta zones only after adjacent zones remain reliable:
- Decatur
- Smyrna/Vinings
- Perimeter/Dunwoody
- Sandy Springs

By Day 90, Atlanta should have a documented, measurable operating template that can be cloned into Market 2.

## Expansion gates

### RED
- no real verified supply
- payment/payout health not ready
- insufficient service coverage

### YELLOW
- supply exists but peak coverage or quality metrics are below target
- controlled beta only

### GREEN
- supply target met
- live coverage target met
- fulfillment >= 90% rolling 7 days
- median ETA <= 20 minutes
- cancellation <= 8%
- rating >= 4.7
- payment/payout systems healthy

## First operating target

The immediate company target is not downloads. It is:

**15 fully verified real Atlanta Heroes, with dependable live coverage in Midtown, Buckhead and Hartsfield-Jackson Airport.**

Once that exists, S.O.S. can responsibly turn on concentrated customer acquisition.
